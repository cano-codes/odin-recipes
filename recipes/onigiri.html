Onigiri (Japanese Rice Balls)

Description

This easy onigiri recipe is fun to make, too! These rice balls are a staple of Japanese lunchboxes (bento).

You can put almost anything in these rice balls; try substituting grilled salmon, pickled plums, beef, pork, turkey, or tuna with mayonnaise.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups uncooked short-grain white rice
  • 5 ½ cups water, divided
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ cup bonito shavings (dry fish flakes)
  • 2 sheets nori (dry seaweed), cut into ½-inch strips
  • 2 tablespoons sesame seeds

Steps

  1. Wash rice in a mesh strainer until water runs clear. Combine washed rice and 4 ½ cups water in a saucepan; bring to a boil over high heat, stirring occasionally. Reduce heat to low; cover, and simmer until water is absorbed, 15 to 20 minutes. Rest for 15 minutes to allow rice to steam and become tender. Cool cooked rice.

  2. Combine remaining 1 cup water with salt in a small bowl (use to dampen hands before handling rice). Divide cooked rice into 8 equal portions, one portion for each onigiri.

  3. Divide 1 rice portion into 2; create a dimple in 1 halved rice portion and fill with 1 heaping teaspoon bonito flakes. Cover with remaining 1 halved rice portion; press lightly to enclose filling inside rice ball. Gently press rice to shape into a triangle; wrap with a nori strip and sprinkle with sesame seeds. Repeat with remaining rice portions.
